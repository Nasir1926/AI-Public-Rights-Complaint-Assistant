# AI-Public-Rights-Complaint-Assistant
An AI-powered assistant that helps people understand, organize, and report public-service complaints by identifying issues, extracting key information, and suggesting possible reporting channels.
# AI Public Rights & Complaint Assistant

Final project for the Building AI course

## Summary

AI Public Rights & Complaint Assistant is an AI-based tool that helps people understand and organize complaints about public services. It analyzes a user's complaint, identifies the issue category, suggests the relevant department, and provides general guidance on possible next steps.

## Background

Many people face problems with public services such as water supply, electricity, roads, sanitation, transportation, government offices, and other community services. People may not know which department is responsible or how to clearly describe their complaint.

This project aims to make the complaint process easier by using AI to organize information and provide guidance.

The problem is important because:

* People often do not know where to report a problem.
* Complaints may be written without enough useful information.
* People may waste time contacting the wrong department.
* Language and technical knowledge can make reporting difficult.
* A structured complaint can make it easier for the responsible organization to understand the issue.

My motivation for this project comes from an interest in public issues and helping ordinary people communicate their problems more clearly.

## How is it used?

The user would open the application and describe their problem in simple language.

For example:

> "There has been no water supply in our area for three days."

The AI could then:

1. Identify the complaint as a **water supply issue**.
2. Extract important information such as location and duration.
3. Ask the user for missing information.
4. Suggest the type of department or service provider that may be responsible.
5. Generate a clear and organized complaint that the user can submit.
6. Provide general information about possible next steps.

The system could support both English and Urdu so that more people can use it.

The main users would be members of the public who need help reporting community or public-service problems.

The system should not make decisions on behalf of government departments. Its role would be to help users organize information and understand possible reporting options.

## Data sources and AI methods

The project could use several types of data:

* Public information about government departments and services.
* Publicly available complaint categories.
* Examples of properly structured complaints.
* Information provided directly by users.
* Public datasets about community and service issues, where available.

Possible AI techniques include:

* **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** to understand user complaints.
* **Text classification** to categorize complaints.
* **Information extraction** to identify locations, dates, services, and other important details.
* **Recommendation systems** to suggest relevant departments or reporting channels.
* **Language processing** to support English and Urdu.
* **Generative AI** to help convert an informal complaint into a clear and structured format.

A simple example of the process is:

```text
User complaint
      ↓
AI understands the text
      ↓
Identify complaint category
      ↓
Extract important information
      ↓
Find possible responsible department
      ↓
Generate structured complaint
      ↓
User reviews and submits it
```

## Challenges

The project would have several limitations.

First, AI may misunderstand a complaint or classify it incorrectly. Therefore, users should always review the generated information before submitting it.

The system would also depend on the quality and availability of public information. Government departments, contact information, and procedures can change over time.

Privacy would be another important concern. Users should not be encouraged to provide unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

The system would not be able to guarantee that a complaint will be solved. It would only help users understand and organize their problem.

The AI should also avoid presenting unverified accusations as facts. If a complaint involves another person or organization, the system should distinguish between what the user reports and what has been independently verified.

## What next?

In the future, the project could be expanded into a complete public-service assistance platform.

Possible future features include:

* Urdu and other local-language support.
* Voice input for people who have difficulty typing.
* Automatic detection of the user's general service area.
* Integration with official complaint portals where available.
* Complaint status tracking.
* Duplicate complaint detection.
* Anonymous reporting options where legally and technically appropriate.
* A dashboard showing common categories of public-service problems.
* Data visualization to help communities understand recurring issues.

To develop the project further, I would need programming skills, access to reliable public datasets, knowledge of NLP and machine learning, and collaboration with people who understand public-service systems.
